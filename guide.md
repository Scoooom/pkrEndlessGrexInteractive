# Endless Entrainment Normalize Build

Guide by Grex. Source: https://wiki.pokerogue.net/guides:endless_normalize_build
This is a restructured, paraphrased copy. `guide.json` holds the same data in machine form.

**Idea:** give the boss Normalize through Entrainment so its attacks cannot hurt you, then whittle it down. Four fused team slots.

## Pros and cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Little to no egg move investment | Needs two speed-invested Pokémon |
| Can farm Master Balls at any time | Needs hidden abilities, mainly Teddiursa and Regigigas |
| Regigigas carries for a long stretch | Heavy splicer usage |
| Almost never fails a shiny catch | Needs a wild Mat Block user |
| Four slots, no wasted abilities | Full completion needs one wild hidden ability Golurk |
| No Protect TM needed | |

## In the run

**Standard boss.** Killer Support goes first: Tera Ghost, Entrainment on the boss, then Curse. Swap to Boss Killer and use its moves until the boss looks like it dies next turn or each move has been used once. Bring in Staller so Triple Lure is active and the next wave opens as a double battle.

**Complicated boss (Slither Wing).** Use only Boss Killer and open with Leech Seed to heal each turn. When the boss is about to die, let it knock out one of your Pokémon so Staller enters safely. Staller can use Mat Block or Dark Void for a couple of turns if needed.

**Capturing safely.** Roar away the Pokémon you do not want, Normalize the one you want, then throw regular balls. Ruination damages it and Dark Void puts it to sleep. Slow, but saves Master Balls and works on bosses.

**Capturing on XX0 waves.** Trick-or-Treat turns the target Ghost type, making it immune to its partner's spread moves. After Normalize, all your Pokémon are immune to both opponents within two turns, which cuts stalling time.
